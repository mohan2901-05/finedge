How it works

Two journeys, one marketplace. These are intended product flows — not all features are live today.

01

Borrower journey

  • Explore financing options and indicative terms.
  • Estimate your monthly repayment with the EMI calculator.
  • Submit a loan application and track its status.
02

Financier journey

  • Review incoming loan requests.
  • Evaluate proposed terms and applicant details.
  • Manage approved and disbursed financing activity.

Built for the marketplace

FinEdge is designed to bring clarity and structure to loan matching between borrowers and financiers.

Indicative terms

Compare loan amounts, tenures, and rates before applying — no hidden figures.

Application tracking

Borrowers can follow their application from submission through review and decision.

Portfolio oversight

Financiers can track approved loans, disbursements, and repayments in one place.
